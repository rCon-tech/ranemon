# The London Ranedex (plan)

A plan only, written 24 October 2026. Nothing here is built yet. It turns Rich's list of 99 London characters into **151 new Ranemon** for the London expansion (`docs/london-expansion.md`, built in PR #62), with three new types, names in the house style, evolution lines, where each one lives in London, and the order to build them in. Every new place in it is **2D only**, like the rest of Britain.

## The short version

- **151 new species in 75 lines**: 13 lines of three, 45 of two, 16 single-stage Ranemon (10 ordinary, a rare fossil and 5 legends) and one Eevee-style Ranemon with five branches. 151 is a nod to Kanto, with a Mew of our own as #151.
- **94 of the 99 characters are in.** 5 are cut, and 19 lines merge two or more characters, as evolutions or as a joke in the Ranedex entry (below).
- **Three new types: Posh, Graft and Arty**, making nine. The chart stays fair: every type hits 3 types for double, is resisted by 3, is weak to 3 and resists 3.
- **Names follow the lads' pattern.** Each base form is one London word plus _-mon_ (Oystermon, Oatmon). Each evolution blends that word with a Gen 1 or 2 Pokémon, and a whole line keeps to one Pokémon family, like Carlizard, Lorchu and Robinsey do.
- **UK Ranemon get evolutions.** The 20 lads' UK forms still don't evolve (that stays as it is). The new London Ranemon evolve at their own levels, from Lv12 to Lv55, instead of everyone at Lv30.
- **All drawn, no photos**, with the pixel-art body kit from the drawn Ranemon (PR #59's branch), 96 px, with a back view, shiny and follower each.
- **Eleven London areas**, opened as you collect jerseys, with wild levels running from Lv3 at Liverpool Street to Lv48 in the City before the palace, and Lv50 to 70 after the credits.
- **Built in six batches** of about 25 to 35 species, each its own PR, with a sprite sheet for Rich to approve before any code goes in.

## What "150" means here

Rich asked to "finish with roughly 150 Ranemon". This plan reads that as **a London Ranedex of about 150 new species, counting every evolution** (the way Kanto's 151 counts Bulbasaur, Ivysaur and Venusaur separately). The 20 lads, their evolutions, Cholokill, Petchemon, Poseidon and Tobymon stay as they are and aren't counted.

If Rich meant 150 _in total_ including the 46 or so that exist, the plan shrinks to about 105 new species: drop the 17 single-stage Ranemon except the legends, and turn about 15 of the three-stage lines into two-stage ones. If he meant 150 _base forms_, it can't come from this list alone (there are 99 characters), so that would need a second list.

## Three new types

The six types are personalities, not elements, and that's why the lads fit them. London's characters mostly do too, but three things keep coming up in the list that none of the six covers: **money**, **work** and **taste**. So:

| --------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |

### How they fit the chart

The existing chart is fair by design: every type beats 2, is resisted by 2, and so on. With nine types, every type now **beats 3, is resisted by 3, is weak to 3 and resists 3**. Nothing between the existing six types changes. Each old type picks up exactly one new strength, one new weakness, and one new resistance on each side, all against the new types.

The new matchups, and why:

- **Posh beats Charm, Brawn and Graft.** Seen every charmer before, hires security, owns the business.
- **Graft beats Chill, Brains and Arty.** Out-works the chill, fixes what the theorist can't, and the artist needs a day job.
- **Arty beats Hype, Chaos and Posh.** Sneers at the mainstream, turns chaos into an installation, and satire is how you beat money.
- **Posh is weak to Chaos, Hype and Arty** (a stag party in the members' club, a mob, a satirist). **Graft is weak to Brawn, Charm and Posh** (a bigger lad, a sweet-talker asking a favour, the boss). **Arty is weak to Brains, Chill and Graft** (the critic, the shrug, the landlord).
- **Stand-offs.** Posh and Brains/Chill resist each other: money doesn't impress them, and they don't trouble money. Graft and Hype/Chaos resist each other. Arty and Brawn/Charm resist each other.
- **Among the new three:** Posh beats Graft beats Arty beats Posh, and each resists itself, like the old types.

The new types share two of their three strengths with an old one (Posh with Brawn, Graft with Brains, Arty with Hype), but their weaknesses and resistances are all different, so they play differently on defence. That's the honest cost of adding three types to a six-type wheel without changing the six.

**Not added: a Foodie type.** About a dozen characters are food (cheese, sourdough, bagels, curry, kebabs, fudge). They're in, but typed by personality (Charm for the cheese tourist, Hype for the curry, Chaos for the kebab) rather than a fourth new type. A tenth type breaks the 3-and-3 balance and needs another round of new moves for every type.

### Type spread

Every type gets between 22 and 31 Ranemon (counting each type of a dual-type Ranemon): Chill 31, Graft 30, Hype 30, Brains 29, Charm 28, Brawn 25, Posh 22, Arty 22, Chaos 22. The lads lean Hype, Charm and Brawn, so London leans the other way.

## Names

The lads' pattern, kept:

1. **Base form: one London word + -mon.** Oystermon, Oatmon, Fleamon, Punkmon. Short, says the joke, and reads as a Ranemon.
2. **Evolutions: that word blended into a Gen 1 or 2 Pokémon**, the way Carl + Charizard is Carlizard and Lorca + Raichu is Lorchu. Flat white + Flaaffy is **Flaatwhite**, then roast + Ampharos is **Ampharoast**.
3. **One Pokémon family per line**, so the line reads as a family: Mareep → Flaaffy → Ampharos gives Oatmon → Flaatwhite → Ampharoast; Zubat → Golbat → Crobat gives Oystermon → Golpeak → Crowdbat; Pidgey → Pidgeotto → Pidgeot gives Pedalmon → Pedgeotto → Pedgeot.
4. **The Pokémon fits the person.** The commuter is the Zubat (in every tunnel, forty of them). The barrister is the owl (Noctwig). The civil servant is Quagsire (blank, unbothered, outlasts every minister: Quangsire). The Elephant & Castle salsa dancer is Phanpy (Donphango). The Lewisham night bus legend is Snorlax (Snorbus). The pseudo-legendary is the hedge funder, on Dratini's line (Hedgonair, Yachtonite).
5. **No family the lads already use.** Charmander, Abra, Clefairy, the Hitmons, Gastly, Magikarp, Machop, Mankey, Totodile, Pichu, Spearow, Onix, Chansey, Koffing, Voltorb, Poliwag, Bulbasaur and Shedinja are all left alone. Two families are used twice on purpose, as the games branch them: Oddish (Bellosoca and Vilekale) and Meowth (Meowford and Persington).
6. **Avoided:** Jynx, whatever the line.
7. **Single-stage Ranemon** are a straight blend with no -mon form: Bitto, Snorbus, Mr. Busk, Supercarmory.

Every name is checked against every Ranemon that exists today, and all 151 are different.

## Evolutions

Today every Ranemon evolves once, at Lv30, into a form with a 460 stat budget, and the UK forms of the lads don't evolve at all. The lads stay that way. The London Ranemon change it:

- **Three-stage lines** (13): evolve twice, early lines at about Lv14 to 20 and Lv30 to 36, late lines later.
- **Two-stage lines** (45): evolve once, anywhere from Lv22 to Lv46. A line found late evolves late, so its base form is still out in the grass where it lives.
- **Single stage** (10, plus the fossil and the 5 legends): never evolve, so they get a bigger budget to make up for it.
- **The Hackney Wick Warehouse Roommate** (Wickmon) is the Eevee: it evolves when you hand it one of five London items, into who the flatmate turned into.
- **Medalian**, the Chelsea Pensioner, evolves at Lv45 from a young soldier, on purpose. The red coat comes with age.

### Stat budgets

The game checks that every Ranemon's stats add up to exactly 400 (`STAT_BUDGET`), and scales evolutions to 460. London needs a budget per stage:

| Stage                         | Budget              | Like                                                                                    |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| First of three                | 320                 | Caught at Lv3 to 12, weaker than a lad, and it should be                                |
| Middle of three               | 400                 | A lad                                                                                   |
| Last of three                 | 470                 | A touch over an evolved lad, for the patience                                           |
| Base of two                   | 360 early, 400 late | Lines found after Lv30 start at a lad's 400                                             |
| Evolved of two                | 460                 | An evolved lad                                                                          |
| Single stage                  | 440                 | Never evolves, so it sits between                                                       |
| Eevee base / branches         | 330 / 460           |                                                                                         |
| Fossil (Aerodactivist)        | 460                 |                                                                                         |
| Pseudo-legendary (Yachtonite) | 490                 | Lv55 to evolve, post-game                                                               |
| Legend trio                   | 480                 | Tobymon's budget                                                                        |
| Mewtube / Mewseum             | 500 / 480           | Post-game only. Tobymon at 520 swept the post-game, so both get the post-game sim first |

The shape of each Ranemon's stats comes from its joke (the speed walker is fast, the doorman has Defense, the civil servant has HP and nothing else), written batch by batch.

The code change for this is in the first batch: per-stage budgets in `validateCreature`, evolution chains of more than one step with a level each, and item evolutions with more than one branch (the Cholokill code already does one).

A few details:

- **The grass is mostly the lads.** Players want a party of the lads, so every wild table, London's included, is at least three quarters lads by weight (`tests/unit/originals.test.ts` checks it). London's own are the rest, area flavour and rarities, and the towns up north (Manchester, Leeds, Liverpool, Birmingham, Newcastle) keep their lad tables as they are.
- **Evolved forms turn up later.** A line's evolved forms appear in later areas at their own levels (Golpeak and Crowdbat are in nearly every tunnel, like Zubat), and the Riverside is mostly evolved forms and rarities.
- **Fiddlemon is a gift.** The W.B Yates London fiddler, who already battles you, hands you one when you beat him. His evolved forms are wild in Kilburn.
- **Rare ones.** Aerodactivist (1 in 100 in Spitalfields), Hedgonair's line (rare in the Docks), Supercarmory (only at night in Knightsbridge), Misgravus (only at dusk in Highgate).
- **The legends.** Strikedos appears once, on a Tube strike day after the Arsenal jersey (Lv45). Drizzticuno and Heatwavtres are post-game (Lv60), in the rain and in the Central line in August. Mewtube is below the deepest platform after the credits (Lv70). Mewseum, #151, is in the British Museum basement once you've caught the other 150 (Lv65), guarding everything that was never theirs to keep; a nod to the Irish Crown Jewels story.
- **Trainers.** London Ranemon are mostly met in trainers' teams. Each new area gets four to six trainers with London Ranemon from that area, and the trainers on the way to the grounds up north each have one of the lads and one Londoner. Everyone who matters (club legends, royals, gym leaders, bosses, the Championship) keeps at least one of the lads. Each of the twelve club legends adds one London Ranemon to their team of lads, from the area that opens around their ground's turn, in the jersey battle itself. There's no extra mission to earn it: every one is catchable in the wild anyway.

## Moves

- **18 shared moves**, six for each new type, in the same shape as the old types (three damaging, one quick move, two status moves). First names: **Posh**: Old Money, Members Only, Trust Fund, Valet (quick), Look Down Your Nose, Second Home (heal). **Graft**: Double Shift, Cash in Hand, Overtime (hits 2 to 5 times), Early Start (quick), Hi-Vis, Tea Break (heal). **Arty**: Masterpiece, Installation, Abstract, Quick Sketch (quick), Pretentious Monologue, Muse.
- **Sport moves**: two or three new ones per new type for the learnsets (polo, Sunday League, synchronised swimming), so London lines learn moves of their own types as they level.
- **Special moves**: one for each line's final form, each of Wickmon's branches and each single stage, 79 in all, named in the roster. Most use the special effects the game already has (boosts, swap HP, hit and run, self-damage). Three need new ones: **Doing a Bit** (Bitto turns into the foe, like Transform), **Rush Hour** (hits once for every Ranemon in your party, like Beat Up) and **Give It Back** (Mewseum takes the foe's held item).
- **Learnsets** for London lines carry over from stage to stage, as the lads' do.

## The Ranedex

- A **London Ranedex**, numbered 001 to 151 in the order above, as its own tab next to the existing Ranedex. The existing dex keeps its order.
- Each species gets **one Ranedex line**, like the lads, written in each batch from the joke in the roster. Funny about the habit, the place and the price of things, never about anyone's ethnicity, religion or accent. That matters for a few on the list (the Golders Green deli regular, the Carnival veteran, the Kilburn fiddler, the corner shop boss, the nail tech, the curry house regular), where the joke is the scene: the queue at 3am, thirty years on the road, the reel at closing time, the shop that's always open.

## Sprites, without photos

There's nothing to cut these from, so all 151 are **drawn pixel art in the PR #59 style**. The body kit (`scripts/drawn/kit.py`, `roster.py`, `costumes.py`, `evolved.py`) is on its branch, not on main yet, so the first batch brings it over.

- **The kit fits.** It draws chibi people dressed from a Look (hair, face, top, pose, props), and every character on the list is a person. Each new Ranemon is a few lines in the kit's roster.
- **New props and clothes** the kit doesn't have yet, added as needed: a pram, a dog lead with several dogs, a road bike and helmet, an oar, a takeaway cup, a skateboard, a ring light, a fiddle, a cleaver, a wig and gown, a hi-vis vest, a clipboard, a paintbrush, a vape, a kebab, swimming cap and goggles.
- **Evolutions look like their Pokémon.** As with Carlizard's wings and Lorchu's tail, each stage draws its family's pieces into the body (Golpeak's bat ears, Crowdbat's four wings, Pedgeot's crest, Kingpond's fins, Jumpouffe's fluff), plus its type glow.
- **Scale.** All sprites stay in the 96 px box. A first stage stands small inside it, a middle stage bigger, a final form fills it, and the legends break the outline a little, as the handhelds do.
- **Per species**: the front, a back view, shiny front and back (the usual gold), and a follower: five images each, **755 in all**.
- **Signed off first.** Each batch makes a contact sheet (fronts, backs, each line side by side) into the project files for Rich to look at before any of it goes in the game.
- **2D only.** No solid 3D figures; they're flat in battles even with the 3D look picked, like the UK forms.

## Balance risks

1. **The bigger chart changes old fights.** Every lad gains a weakness to one new type, and the old trainers' movesets only cover six types. The battle sims (`tests/sim/`) are rerun with nine types in the first batch, and a lad's matchups against the new types are checked before anything ships.
2. **The legends get easier.** With Posh, Graft and Arty Ranemon in your party, the twelve club legends (all lads) have new weaknesses. If the sims show it, each legend gets one London Ranemon.
3. **Budgets above 460.** Yachtonite, the three-stage finals and the legends are over today's ceiling. Tobymon was cut from 520 to 480 for sweeping the post-game, so every one of these goes through the post-game sim before it's in.
4. **A base form that never appears.** If an area's wild levels go past a line's evolution level, only the evolved form shows up. The levels above are set so every base form is catchable where it lives; the sims check each area's table.
5. **Volume.** 151 species, 151 entries, 79 special moves and 755 images is about six times everything in the game today. Batches of 25 to 35 keep each PR reviewable.
6. **Battle mode.** London Ranemon stay in story mode at first. Putting 151 more into the team select and friend battles is a separate decision for later.
7. **Saves.** New species ids are added, never renamed, so existing saves keep working.
8. **The doc's levels.** `docs/london-expansion.md`'s Premier League table (aces 42 to 58) doesn't match the game (10 to 46). This plan follows the game, and batch 0 corrected that table.

## Decided (24 October 2026)

Rich approved the plan as written:

1. **"150" means 151 new species, evolutions included**, not counting the lads.
2. **Three new types: Posh, Graft and Arty.**
3. **The five cuts and the merges** above.
